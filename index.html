Ball Jump: meet the ball with your feet while it is rising from a pull. Releasing the pull still counts while the ball rises. Homing Rocket attacks nearby enemies automatically.

Start with Ball Jump. Rocket unlocks at level 3; Blink at level 6. Unlocked loadouts appear numbered in unlock order, so press 1, then 2, then 3 as your kit grows. The running cooldown continues and blocks every ability until it finishes.
